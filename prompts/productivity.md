# Productivity

5 prompts. See [PROMPTING-GUIDE.md](../PROMPTING-GUIDE.md) for how to write your own.

## Plan My Day in 10 Minutes

**Purpose:** Turn a must-do list and real free hours into a schedule that fits.

```
Plan my day for me. Today is [day/date]. I must do: [list the must-dos]. I have [hours] free. I also want to fit in [one personal thing].

Give me a simple schedule with times, in order. Put the hardest task first, add two short breaks, and leave 30 minutes of buffer. If everything doesn't fit, tell me what to move to tomorrow.
```

**Tip:** List your real must-dos, not your wish list — a plan that pretends you have 12 free hours won't survive contact with reality.

**What to expect:** 08:00-09:30: finish the report (hardest first). 09:30-09:45: break. 10:00: call the supplier...

## Break This Big Task Into Small Steps

**Purpose:** Split a big, stuck task into 30-minute steps with a first step you can do in 5 minutes.

```
Break this task into small steps I can actually start:

Task: [describe it]
Deadline: [when]
I have about [hours per day] to work on it.

Give me steps that each take 30-60 minutes, in order, with the first step so small I could do it in 5 minutes. Mark the step where I should check my progress. End with: what's the one thing that would make this fail, and how do I avoid it?
```

**Tip:** If a step still feels big, it's not a step — break it again until it feels easy to start.

**What to expect:** Step 1 (5 min): open a blank doc and write the title. Step 2: list the 5 sections. Step 3: write section 1...

## Help Me Prioritize When Everything Feels Urgent

**Purpose:** Sort a messy task list into do today, do this week, and drop — without mercy.

```
Help me prioritize. Here's my list:

[Paste your task list]

For each task, ask: is it urgent, is it important, and what happens if I don't do it this week? Then sort them into: do today, do this week, delegate or drop. Be ruthless — if something can be dropped, say so and explain why it doesn't matter as much as I think.
```

**Tip:** Add the deadline and the consequence for each task — without those, everything looks equally urgent.

**What to expect:** Do today: reply to the client (deadline tomorrow, blocks payment). Drop: redesign the footer (no one asked for it, no deadline).

## Turn Messy Notes Into a Clean Summary

**Purpose:** Turn raw meeting notes into decisions, action items, and open questions.

```
Turn these meeting notes into a clean summary:

[Paste your raw notes]

Give me: 1) decisions made, 2) action items with owner and deadline (mark anything unclear with [?]), 3) open questions. Keep it under 200 words. Use the names people actually use, not formal titles.
```

**Tip:** Paste the notes right after the meeting — a day later, half the context is gone from your memory.

**What to expect:** Decisions: we launch on Monday with the current design. Actions: Rina — send the price list by Friday...

## Build a Habit I Won't Quit in a Week

**Purpose:** Build a habit starting so small you can't fail, with a plan for the day you miss.

```
Help me build the habit of [habit]. Right now I [current situation]. I can spend [minutes] a day on it.

Give me: a version so small I can't fail at it, when and where I'll do it (tie it to something I already do), how to track it simply, and what to do on the day I miss — because I will miss a day. No motivational speeches, just the plan.
```

**Tip:** Start embarrassingly small — 2 minutes a day beats a 1-hour plan you quit on day three.

**What to expect:** Tiny version: read one page after breakfast. Track: mark an X on the calendar. Missed a day? Just do the tiny version tomorrow, never double up.
