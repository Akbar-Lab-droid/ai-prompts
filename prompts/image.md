# Image Generation

5 prompts. See [PROMPTING-GUIDE.md](../PROMPTING-GUIDE.md) for how to write your own.

## A Realistic Portrait Prompt

**Purpose:** Write an image prompt that produces a portrait that looks like a real photo.

```
Write an image generation prompt for a realistic portrait photo of [describe the person]. Setting: [where]. Light: [natural window light / golden hour / studio].

Make it specific: camera (85mm lens, shallow depth of field), expression, clothing, background. Add what to avoid: no plastic skin, no extra fingers, no weird eyes. Keep the whole prompt under 80 words.
```

**Tip:** Name the lens and light — '85mm, soft window light' changes a portrait more than any adjective.

**What to expect:** A candid portrait of a 30-year-old woman laughing, sitting by a cafe window, soft morning light on her face, 85mm lens, shallow depth of field, warm tones, natural skin texture.

## A Product Photo That Looks Expensive

**Purpose:** Write a product photo prompt with clean light, one surface, and no clutter.

```
Write an image generation prompt for a product photo of [product]. Background: [clean white / lifestyle scene].

Describe: the product's position, the lighting (soft studio light, one shadow), the surface it's on, and the mood. Add: photorealistic, high detail, no text, no logos, no extra objects. Under 60 words.
```

**Tip:** One product, one surface, one light direction — cluttered product shots look cheap.

**What to expect:** A matte black wireless router on a light wooden table, soft studio light from the left, gentle shadow, minimal background, photorealistic, sharp detail.

## Logo Concepts to Show a Designer

**Purpose:** Get logo ideas with shapes and colors you can hand to a designer as a brief.

```
Give me 5 logo concepts for a business called [name] that does [what it does]. Audience: [who]. Style: [modern / playful / premium].

For each concept: one sentence describing the idea, the main shape or symbol, and 2 colors with hex codes. Then tell me which one you'd pick and why. These are ideas to brief a designer, not final logos.
```

**Tip:** A logo brief works when it says what the business does in one line — designers can't read your mind.

**What to expect:** Concept 1: a signal wave forming a house roof — teal #0D9488 + dark navy #0F172A.

## An Illustration in a Specific Style

**Purpose:** Write an illustration prompt with a real palette and a clear feeling.

```
Write an image generation prompt for an illustration of [subject] in the style of [style, e.g. flat vector, watercolor, retro poster].

Describe: the main elements, the color palette (3 colors), the composition, and the feeling it should give. Add: clean lines, no text, no watermark. Under 70 words.
```

**Tip:** Name 3 colors exactly — 'blue and orange' is vague, '#2563EB, #F97316, #F8FAFC' is a palette.

**What to expect:** Flat vector illustration of a person working from a rooftop garden at sunset, teal, orange and cream palette, simple shapes, calm mood, no text.

## A Thumbnail People Actually Click

**Purpose:** Write a thumbnail prompt with one face, one object, and room for text.

```
Write an image generation prompt for a YouTube thumbnail about [video topic]. The video is for [audience].

Describe: one clear focal image, a face showing [emotion], bold contrast, and space on the left for text. Style: bright, high contrast, 16:9. No text in the image itself — I'll add that later. Under 60 words.
```

**Tip:** One face, one object, one emotion — thumbnails with three ideas get ignored.

**What to expect:** Close-up of a surprised man holding a router, bright yellow background, strong contrast, left third empty for text, 16:9.
